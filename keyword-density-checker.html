How the tool works

Keyword density checker: spot repetition and gaps

How do we keep it free?

This is a small side project, and we keep it free for everyone. Your text is never stored or sold.

Paste your content, choose one, two or three word phrases, and read the table. Common words like the and and are skipped so the list shows meaningful terms. Type a target keyword to get its exact density with a plain verdict. Most natural pages sit somewhere between 0.5% and 2% for their main phrase.

If your top phrases do not match your topic, the content may be drifting. Add related terms, synonyms and answers to the questions people actually ask instead of repeating the exact phrase. A good test is to read the page aloud. If it sounds forced, trim the repeats. Modern search engines understand meaning, so clear and helpful writing will always do more for you than hitting a target number.

What is a good keyword density?

There is no official number. Around 0.5% to 2% for the main phrase usually reads naturally.

Does keyword stuffing hurt rankings?

Yes. Repeating a phrase unnaturally can make a page read poorly and may be treated as spam.
