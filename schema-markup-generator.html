How the tool works




Schema generator: help search engines understand your page


Schema markup is a short piece of structured data that tells search engines what your page is about in a language they understand. Article, FAQ, LocalBusiness and Product are among the most useful types. With correct markup a page can become eligible for rich results such as star ratings, prices or business details, although Google decides when and whether to show them.


Choose a type, fill in the fields and copy the code. Paste it into the head of your page, or just before the closing body tag, then check it with Google's Rich Results Test. The code is JSON-LD, the format Google recommends. The values must match what visitors can really see on the page, or the markup may be ignored.


Start with the type that fits your page best, and keep the details accurate and up to date, especially prices and opening hours. Note that Google now shows FAQ rich results only for a limited set of sites, but the markup still helps machines understand your content. Add one schema block per page topic rather than copying the same block everywhere.


Where do I paste the schema code?
Inside the head of your page, or just before the closing body tag. Most CMS plugins also have a custom code box.


Does schema guarantee rich results?
No. It makes your page eligible, but Google decides whether to show them.
