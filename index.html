Yes. Every tool is free to use as often as you like, with no signup and no payment.


What is the best meta title length?
Aim for 50–60 characters, which usually stays under 580 pixels on desktop.


What is the best meta description length?
Between 140 and 160 characters. Google may rewrite it, but a well-written one still earns more clicks.


What keyword density is safe?
There is no magic number. Around 1–2% reads naturally. Write for people first.
