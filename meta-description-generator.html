How the tool works

Title and description generator: skip the blank page

Staring at an empty box is the slowest part of writing meta tags. The generator gives you several ready options from one keyword, a brand name and a tone, so you can pick a direction and then polish it in your own words. Friendly sounds like a helpful guide, Professional suits business pages, and Action-driven pushes the click with a clear next step.

Each title is kept to 60 characters or fewer, and the brand name is added only when there is room. Descriptions are fitted between 140 and 160 characters, with the exact count beside every result. Press Copy, paste it into your CMS, then run it through the meta checker to see how it looks in a search result.

Treat the output as a first draft. Add one real detail from your page, such as a number, a year, a price or a city, because specific lines get more clicks than general ones. Do not use the same description on many pages, and mention the keyword once or twice at most. If you are unsure about tone, start with Friendly for blogs, Action-driven for shops and tools, and Professional for services like law, finance or consulting. Always write for the person reading the result, not for the algorithm.

Can I use the generated text as it is?

You can, but it works best when you add one specific detail from your page and adjust the wording to your voice.

Does the generator need an internet connection?

No. It runs in your browser and nothing you type is sent to a server.
